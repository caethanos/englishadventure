# English Adventure Pilot — v1.1 changes

## Included
- More natural, conversational Brazilian Portuguese in key Week 1 instructions and translations.
- Day 1 writing activity with beginner-friendly, rule-based feedback for introduction structures.
- Browser microphone recording, playback, and local audio download.
- Clear notice that recordings are not automatically uploaded or sent to the teacher.
- Week 1 cultural story about The Beatles' 1964 US visit, with A1 English, Portuguese translation, vocabulary, synthesized audio, and a comprehension question.
- Song of the Week links for searching/listening through YouTube, Spotify, and Musixmatch; no copyrighted lyrics are copied into the app.
- More flexible answer acceptance for the Day 2 retrieval prompt.
- Learning streak/offensive remains a roadmap item only; it has not been implemented.

## Known limitations
- Writing feedback is rule-based and narrow. It is not AI correction and should be treated as an initial scaffold.
- Audio is created in the browser and downloaded by the learner. There is no upload, teacher inbox, cloud storage, or automatic speech assessment.
- Progress and pilot notes are stored in localStorage on the current browser/device.
- Song links open search results; availability can vary by country and service.
